Yeah I’d say it’s useful, but not in the “set it and forget it” way people hype it up.

Tools like OpenClaw are great for automating boring repeatable tasks and showing what agent workflows could look like. But for real professional work, you still need supervision because one wrong assumption can snowball fast.

Right now agents are more like junior interns. Fast, helpful, sometimes impressive, but you don’t let them run production without checks.

So not pure hype, but definitely early stage. The real value is in assisted execution, not autonomous decision making.
